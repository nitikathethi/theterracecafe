Meet Your Trainer
AK
Ashish Singh Avsharan Singh Khurana
Founder & Head Barista, The Terrace Cafe · Kopri, Thane East

Hey! I'm Ashish, the owner of The Terrace Cafe — and I've spent years perfecting every drink on our menu. My journey with coffee began during my time studying and working in New Zealand, where I was immersed in one of the world's most respected café cultures.

New Zealand is where I truly learned to make best-in-class coffee — from mastering espresso extraction to understanding the nuances of milk texture, latte art, and what separates an average cup from an exceptional one. That experience changed how I see coffee forever.

I brought that knowledge back and built The Terrace Cafe in Kopri, Thane East — because I believed this neighbourhood deserved a truly great cup. Now I want to share that craft with you.

In this session, you'll operate a real coffee machine, practice espresso extraction, froth milk, and attempt latte art. Ask me anything — and leave knowing something you genuinely can't learn from a YouTube video.
